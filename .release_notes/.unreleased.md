## Bug Fixes

- Telemetry is now best-effort and never crashes a storage operation. Provider/exporter construction failures disable metrics/traces (and cache the disabled state); a failing metric record latches metrics off for the storage client instead of raising; and metrics init is skipped entirely when `MSC_TELEMETRY_DISABLED` or `OTEL_SDK_DISABLED` is set. The `_otlp_mtls_vault` exporter also fails fast with a clear error when the installed OTLP exporter is too old for its mTLS client-certificate options.
